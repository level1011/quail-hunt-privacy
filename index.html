Quail Hunt – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Package: ge.quailhunt.game

Quail Hunt is a bird-hunting game for Android. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifier. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Game progress

Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Two-player online mode

If you choose to play with a partner online, your device connects to our relay server, which passes game messages (positions of the hunters, dogs and birds, shots, scores) between the two players. To make that connection the server necessarily sees your IP address, as any internet service does. The IP address is used only while you are connected, to deliver the messages and to protect the server from abuse. Game messages are not stored. Standard technical server logs may be kept by our hosting provider (Google Cloud) for a limited time. The connection is encrypted in transit. Single-player games do not use the server.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Children

The game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact e-mail shown on the game's Google Play page.
